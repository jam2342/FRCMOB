# Code of Conduct

FRCMOB follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, constructive, and mindful that many contributors and users are students. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-advisory channel. Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy as far as practical and respond proportionately and consistently.
